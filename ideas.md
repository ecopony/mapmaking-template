# Ideas — raw capture

Plop ideas here fast, unpolished. No required fields. When one firms up, promote it to a
brief in `projects/` (copy `templates/project-brief.md`). Group related maps however you
like — a shared tag, or a `series-<name>.md` file for a multi-map arc.

> These are placeholder examples to show the shape. Delete them and add your own.

---

## Example ideas

- **Elevation zones of a mountain range.** Threshold a DEM into bands (foothills,
  montane, subalpine, alpine) and render them as colored zones. *(Promoted to
  `projects/example-elevation-zones/` as the worked example — see its `brief.md`.)*

- **Rivers sized by discharge.** A reference map where line weight encodes mean annual
  flow rather than a uniform stroke, so the big rivers actually look big.

- **Night-sky darkness.** Light-pollution raster classified into Bortle-ish bands over a
  terrain backdrop; where you can still see the Milky Way.
